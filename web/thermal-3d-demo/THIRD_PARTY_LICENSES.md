## Map data

Building footprints in `public/data/buildings.json` are derived from OpenStreetMap via Overpass API.

- © OpenStreetMap contributors
- License: [Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL)](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)
- Massing extrusion, roof details, and cantilever accents are original demo geometry, not OSM 3D models.

## Imagery

Curated JPG textures under `public/textures/` are downsampled stills from Murat Turkeli’s UAV thermography campaigns (Autel EVO II Dual-style `MAX_*` / `IRX_*`). They are included for this research / educational demo only. Do not treat them as a public domain photo pack.

No Sketchfab / Wikimedia GLB for Richard Weeks Hall or CoRE was found with a clear redistributable license suitable for this repo. Campus Crusher / Mini Skyline visualizations cite OSM but are not drop-in GLB sources for school embed.
